three rules that keep this from turning into a feed
The rack holds four
A fifth pushes the oldest off. Scarcity does the curating; there is no ranking and no archive to scroll.
No caption field
You leave the page itself. The line under it is the page's own title — not your take on it.
Taking goes to the site
Not to a saved list. Take it and you are on the page, the way Stumble worked.
the act
One click leaves the page you arrived from. One click takes one down and rides it to the site. This is the closest thing here to a Stumble button — but the queue is other riders, not an algorithm.
at zero riders
Weakest of the five — an empty rack is just empty. This is the one that needs seeding from your own curation until traffic carries it.
