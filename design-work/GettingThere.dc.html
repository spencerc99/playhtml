no menus — three places in the car
1
Any seat → stand next to it and the prompt gains a second verb: sit · look under. Look under opens 01.
2
Window bays — five, not just the door wall: a seat slot in the middle of each bank gives way to glass, and the two open gaps on the lower wall are glazed too. Stand in any dashed gap: wipe the glass. Opens 02, one pane per bay — no seat is more than two seats from a window.
3
The strap bands → walk under one: grab hold. Opens 06 in place — no camera move, you just hang.
All three reuse the plumbing the car already has: the seat hover prompt, the one contextual mobile action, walk-into-it triggering. Doors keep exit; nothing competes with leaving.
