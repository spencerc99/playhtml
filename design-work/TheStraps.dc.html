The straps

grab hold · swing · the train does the rest
you
strap to strap
the view tilts for everyone
riding
Straps sway on the car's existing 1.7s rumble. Your swing adds on top of it — pump in time and you go higher.
arriving
The brake throws every hanging rider forward at once — the one shared slapstick moment per stop, nobody triggers it.
stopped
Everything hangs dead still. Swinging at a platform feels conspicuous — which is correct.
swinging together
Three or more riders pumping in rhythm and the whole car starts to rock — the window landscape tilts for everyone, sitters included. The payoff is public: you can't tell from inside a feed that other people are here right now, but you can feel a rocking train. Emotes still work while hanging; this doesn't replace the wheel, it gives your body something the wheel can't.
two more realtime verbs, unbuilt
Pole rhythm — tap the handrail pole; taps quantize to the rumble so any two people accidentally make a beat. The music idea, without instruments.
Floor-light hopscotch — the two floor circles light when stood on; two riders on both at once makes the aisle glow. A reason to cross the car toward a stranger.
the act
Walk under a strap, grab hold, pump with clicks or keys. No camera move, no mode — you're still in the car, still visible, still able to let go and walk to your door. Leaves no trace; it's the realtime complement to 01 and 02.
isn't this just sitting?
Sitting is parked presence — the seat holds you while your attention leaves for the site. A strap only moves while you pump it, so a swinging strap is proof someone is here right now. Enforce that: the strap goes dead the instant input stops. If it still reads as a third parking spot, the fallback is to cut grab hold as a chosen verb and make it the automatic standing state under a band — swinging becomes something you discover by wiggling, not an activity you select.
