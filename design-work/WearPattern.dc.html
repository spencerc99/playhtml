why this one matters most
This is the only activity with no activity. Sitting is already what you do. The history accumulates from presence alone, which means it works at any scale of participation — including people who never engage with anything.
the act
Choose a seat. That's it. Over months the car develops a shape — a worn aisle line, a favorite window seat, a bank nobody uses — that is legibly the product of everyone who rode.
at zero riders
Strongest of the five. Wear is cumulative and never resets, so an empty car is still full of evidence. The fewer people, the more each visit shows.
